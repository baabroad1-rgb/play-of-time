How it works

  1. 1
    Press RUNThe stopwatch starts.
  2. 2
    Press STOP at 3.000Right after the press you see how far off you were.
  3. 3
    RepeatA fixed series of attempts, the same for everyone, so results compare fairly.

What is measured

AccuracyHow close your presses come to 3.000 seconds.
Early or lateWhether you tend to stop before the mark or after it.
SteadinessHow evenly you hold your level from one attempt to the next.
CorrectionHow you adjust after a miss in one direction.
TempoYour natural rhythm between attempts.

Measurement only

This is the measuring core of Play of Time with everything else taken out: levels, quests, scores and rankings stay in the game. Every attempt is recorded the same way, which keeps one measurement comparable with another.

Anonymous. You get a random code instead of an account. Your attempts are stored under this code and used only for the measurement.
